# Synthetic data

Everything here is generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` with a fixed seed, so every run
produces the same files. All borrowers, employers, banks, appraisers, title companies,
addresses and account numbers are invented, and the lender, Halvaren Home Lending, is
fictional. Every PDF carries a footer that says it is synthetic. The business date is
November 19, 2026 (the `CONFIG` table in Snowflake).

| File | Rows | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `csv/loans.csv` | 60 | Loans with the borrower-stated facts, stage, target closing date and processor |
| `csv/documents.csv` | 512 | Intake manifest linking each PDF to a loan. `labeled_doc_type` is used only to measure AI_CLASSIFY |
| `csv/conditions.csv` | 115 | Underwriting conditions (the pipeline adds rule-based ones) |
| `csv/qc_findings.csv` | 158 | Twelve months of post-closing QC reviews of other, closed loans |
| `documents/*.pdf` | 512 | Application summaries, pay stubs, W-2s, bank statements, appraisal summaries, title commitments, closing disclosures |

Loans by stage: 8 Application, 13 Processing, 14 Underwriting, 13 Conditional approval, 6 Clear to close, 6 Closed. Later stages have more documents (6 to 10 per loan). Appraisals ordered recently may not have arrived yet, so some loans show pending checks.
